06Questions

Questions brands ask us.

We already have automations. Why does it still feel like we’re doing everything?

Because most setups automate the steps but not the watching. Someone still has to notice when a flow stops, remember the edge cases and fix things when apps change. We take over that part too, so the work actually leaves your plate.

What exactly do you build?

A custom system built around how your business works, so no two builds look the same. It can cover the whole flow: from the moment an order comes in, through warehouse, delivery, installation and customer updates, to asking for a review and checking that your service landed well. We only automate the parts that are worth it, and simplify or remove the rest.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built in your own Shopify, helpdesk and automation accounts, with documentation your team can read.

What happens when something breaks?

Our checks flag it, we fix it, and you get a short note explaining what happened and what changed. Fixes for things we monitor are included in your plan.

Can we cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, with 30 days’ notice. Because everything lives in your accounts with documentation, you keep it all.

Tell us what you still check by hand.

A free 30-minute call. We’ll tell you honestly what can run on its own, what should be simplified, and what isn’t worth automating. The in-depth review of your setup happens in the paid audit.
