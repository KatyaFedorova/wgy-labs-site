# WGY Labs: call cheat sheet

## In one line
We automate the day-to-day operations of e-commerce brands, cut the steps that aren't needed, and check every flow daily, so nothing fails quietly. The founder stops being the reminder system.

## Who it's for
- Shopify and other e-commerce brands with physical products, roughly $50k+/month revenue.
- Best fit: several parties per order (warehouse/3PL, courier, installer, factory), a small team, and a founder still holding operations together.
- Strongest niche: bulky or installed products (like Easi Breezi).

## Services
- **Build**: operations automation (orders, warehouse/3PL, delivery, installation, customer updates, reviews), plus AI support agents for order status, returns and edits. AI only where it earns its place.
- **Test**: test sets from real tickets, re-testing on every prompt/model change, health checks of existing Gorgias / Shopify Flow / AI agent setups.
- **Monitor**: daily checks, alerts when anything stops or drifts, fixes when apps change, monthly report.

## How we work (5 stages)
1. **Audit week**: with the people who do the work, find where the hours and money go.
2. **Your plan**: map of today + ranked list of changes. Theirs whether or not we build.
3. **Build in phases**: one flow at a time, each sized to pay for itself.
4. **Test and launch**: on real data, alongside their team, human approval wherever money is involved.
5. **Support and maintenance** (optional): keep it running, adapt it as the business changes.

**Each flow:** Map → Choose (2–3 options compared on upkeep, running cost, risk, time) → Release live → Adjust → Next flow.

## The 30-minute call (fit call, not free consulting)
1. **2 min**: thanks, agenda, "I'll ask a few questions, then tell you honestly if and how we can help."
2. **15 min**: discovery
   - Walk me through what happens after an order comes in.
   - What do you or your team still check, copy or chase by hand? How many hours a week?
   - What's broken or failed quietly in the last few months? How did you find out?
   - Which tools: Shopify, 3PL, helpdesk, email, WhatsApp, Zoho, Zapier/Make/n8n?
   - Who keeps it all running today? What happens when they're away?
   - Were you thinking of hiring for this?
3. **8 min**: what we'd do: likely first flow, what could be removed rather than automated, rough fit with pricing.
4. **5 min**: next step. Propose the **$750 audit** (1 week, credited to the build). Agree a start date. Send a short follow-up email the same day.

## Don't do on the call
- Don't design the full solution for free: that's the audit.
- Don't promise exact savings before the audit.
- Don't overstate experience or client numbers.
