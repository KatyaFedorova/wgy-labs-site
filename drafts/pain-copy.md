## 3. The hidden job [BLACK]

Heading: **You never hired anyone for this job. But someone's doing it: you.**

Three columns:
- **You're the reminder system.** The steps live in your head, and the business works because you don't forget them.
- **You're the monitoring.** Nothing tells you when a flow stops. You find out when a customer asks where their order is.
- **You're the backup.** When an app updates, a courier cancels or a field changes name, it lands on your desk, usually at the worst time.

Closing line:
> It's not a lot of work each time. It's a little bit of work, all the time, and it never fully switches off.

## 6. What changes [WHITE]

Heading: **Close the laptop. It's handled.**
